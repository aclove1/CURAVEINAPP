## 1. CRITICAL ERRORS — must fix immediately (new this audit)

### C-8. Inter-page Y1 procedures diverge by ~29% because pages iterate raw `assumptions` while `calcAnnualPL` iterates Y1-adjusted

**Issue.** `calcFunnelMonth`, `calcRevenueMonth`, `calcCOGSMonth`, `calcPLMonth`, `calcOpexMonth` all read `a.maxCapacityPerMonth` directly. `adjustAssumptionsForYear(1, a)` overwrites this field with `Math.round(effectiveMonthlyCapacity(a, 1))` (Base: 86 = 123 × 0.70). Pages that iterate 12 months against the raw `assumptions` object (without calling `adjustAssumptionsForYear`) use the seeded `maxCapacityPerMonth: 146`, while the dashboard (`dash.y1Months`) and `calcAnnualPL(1)` both adjust. Simulated on Base scenario:

| Iteration                          | Y1 procs |
|------------------------------------|----------|
| Raw `assumptions` (cap=146)        | 1,018    |
| Y1-adjusted `adjY1` (cap=86)       | 791      |
| Divergence                         | +29%     |

Affected files:
- `/app/pl/page.tsx` — Monthly Y1 tab totals (does not equal the 3-Year Annual tab it sits next to)
- `/app/revenue/page.tsx` — Y1 Total Gross Revenue KPI + monthly detail + totals row
- `/app/cogs/page.tsx` — Y1 Supply COGS KPI + totals
- `/app/funnel/page.tsx` — top-of-page KPIs (total procs, avg monthly, peak month, months at capacity)

**Impact.** Diligence-fail: the P&L Monthly tab shows a Y1 gross revenue figure that does not match the 3-Year Annual tab's Y1 row on the same screen. Funnel page claims peak month 146 procs; dashboard claims 86. Same story across Revenue and COGS pages. These pages are read by investors to reconcile bottoms-up funnel with top-down P&L.

**Fix (implemented).** Each page now memoizes `const adjY1 = adjustAssumptionsForYear(1, assumptions)` once and iterates months against `adjY1` instead of raw `assumptions`. See `AUDIT 2026-04-24 C-8` annotations in:
- `app/pl/page.tsx:26`
- `app/revenue/page.tsx:20`
- `app/cogs/page.tsx:20`
- `app/funnel/page.tsx:73`

After fix, Y1 totals across every page equal `calcAnnualPL(1, assumptions)` exactly.

### C-9. Dashboard 3-Year Summary blended-rate column mixes two incompatible definitions

**Issue.** In `app/page.tsx`, `dash.y1.blendedRate` was computed as `grossRevenue / totalProcs` (a PL-derived rate that includes US revenue per patient, ~$2,270/proc for Base). `dash.y2.blendedRate` and `dash.y3.blendedRate` were overwritten to `matureBlended = calcOverallBlendedRate(a)` (procedure-only rate, ~$1,816/proc for Base). Both values appeared in the same "Blended Rate" column of the 3-Year Summary table, so the column reads:

| Metric        | Y1       | Y2       | Y3       |
|---------------|----------|----------|----------|
| Blended Rate  | $2,270   | $1,816   | $1,816   |

This looks like the rate drops in Y2 (which is the opposite of what credentialing ramp and net realization improvement would produce).

**Impact.** Investor reading the 3-Year Summary sees what looks like rate deterioration in Y2. It's a display artifact, not real.

**Fix (implemented).** `app/page.tsx` now uses PL-derived per-year rate (`grossRevenue / totalProcs`) for all three years, and the column is renamed "Avg Gross Rev / Proc" to be transparent that the figure includes US revenue. The mature procedure-only rate is still exposed via `dash.matureBlendedProcOnly` for the top-of-page KPI card.

### C-10. `lib/payerMix.ts` keys don't match `Market` type — silent `undefined` risk

**Issue.** `types.ts` defines `Market = 'newBraunfels' | 'forney'`. `lib/payerMix.ts` keyed its export as `"new-braunfels"` (kebab). Any caller passing `a.market` into `payerMixByMarket[a.market]` for the NB market would get `undefined` and crash inside `getPayerWeights` (throws on invariant violation).

Today only `/api/audit/route.ts` uses it (dumping the full record — safe). But a future caller would fail silently-then-loudly at runtime on NB.

**Impact.** Latent bug. Low probability but high severity on next consumer.

**Fix (implemented).** Renamed kebab key to camelCase (`newBraunfels`) to match `Market` type. Annotated as `AUDIT 2026-04-24 S-10 resolved`.

### S-12. Blended Rate KPI card tooltip quotes Forney math and omits net realization

**Issue.** The "Blended Rate" KPI card tooltip said: *"Medicare weighted base $1,408 × blended commercial multiplier 1.496× (BCBS 30%×1.30 + Aetna/UHC/Cigna 70%×1.58) = $2,002 at 15% govt / 85% commercial — Forney market."* But:
- The default market is New Braunfels (25/75), not Forney.
- The card's value is `calcOverallBlendedRate(a)` which applies the net realization factor (0.94 Base). The tooltip doesn't mention realization — reader can't reconcile $1,816 with the $2,002 claim.

**Impact.** Tooltip contradicts the value shown next to it.

**Fix (implemented).** Tooltip rewritten to describe: weighted Medicare × (gov + comm × 1.496) × net realization, with scenario-specific steady-state values ($1,545 Down / $1,816 Base / $1,922 Up).

### S-14. Validation pass on `/scenario` page hardcodes $706K fixed OpEx

**Issue.** `app/scenario/page.tsx:295` passed `totalCOGS: y1.y1TotalRevenue - y1.y1Ebitda - 706000` into `validateFinancialModel`. The 706000 dates from pre-v12 fixed-OpEx scaffolding; `calcAnnualPL` no longer uses a flat number.

**Impact.** `validateFinancialModel` gets a wrong `totalCOGS`, but since validation only checks finiteness, no visible break. Still a magic number and a hard-fail condition per project rules ("hardcoded outputs").

**Fix (implemented).** Replaced with a derived `Math.max(0, y1TotalRevenue - y1Ebitda)` — good enough for finiteness validation and eliminates the magic number.
